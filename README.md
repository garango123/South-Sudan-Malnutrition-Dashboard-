A professional and interactive analytics dashboard that visualizes acute malnutrition trends across South Sudan’s 10 states and 3 Administrative Areas. The platform provides data-driven insights for children, adolescents, and adults through dynamic charts, regional comparisons, and interactive visualizations to support humanitarian response, public health planning, and evidence-based policy decisions.
The dashboard integrates nutrition and food security indicators derived from reports and assessments by WHO, IPC, UNICEF, WFP, and other humanitarian partners supporting South Sudan.
